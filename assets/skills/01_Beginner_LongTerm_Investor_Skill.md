# Beginner Long-Term Investor Skill

## Best fit
- New investor building first rules-based portfolio.
- Horizon: 5+ years.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Markets cycle between risk-on and risk-off.
- Long-term compounding dominates short-term noise.

## Essential rules
1. Build emergency fund before aggressive equity risk.
2. Invest at fixed intervals, regardless of headlines.
3. Prioritize diversified, profitable, quality assets first.
4. Keep speculative allocation small and explicit.
5. Review monthly; rebalance quarterly.

## Risk guard rails
1. Single position <= 10% of portfolio.
2. Speculative bucket <= 15% of portfolio.
3. Use a hard max drawdown plan: reduce risk if portfolio drawdown exceeds 15%.
4. No leverage.
5. No concentration into one theme until full cycle evidence exists.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Is the company/ETF in a persistent long-term uptrend?
2. Are earnings quality and cash flow stable?
3. Is valuation extreme versus own history?
4. Is position size aligned with risk tolerance?

## Execution protocol
1. Weekly: add planned contribution.
2. Monthly: check allocation drift and thesis validity.
3. Quarterly: rebalance back to target weights.

## Failure modes to avoid
- Performance chasing.
- Panic selling quality holdings during routine corrections.
- Changing strategy every month.
