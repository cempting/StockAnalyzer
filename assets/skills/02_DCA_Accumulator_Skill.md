# DCA Accumulator Skill

## Best fit
- Investor optimizing behavior and consistency over precision timing.
- Horizon: 3+ years.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Volatility is opportunity when cashflow is stable.
- Average entry quality improves through disciplined repetition.

## Essential rules
1. Invest fixed amount on fixed schedule.
2. Use predefined asset list only.
3. Double-down only when all preconditions are met.
4. Continue schedule during fear events unless thesis breaks.
5. Keep a cash buffer for exceptional drawdowns.

## Double-down guard rails
1. Trigger only on meaningful drawdown from prior average cost.
2. Asset must remain fundamentally intact.
3. Macro regime must not invalidate thesis.
4. Cap each double-down at predefined multiplier of base contribution.

## Risk guard rails
1. No single asset > 15% after averaging.
2. Pause averaging into structurally broken assets.
3. If thesis fails, stop DCA and reallocate.
4. Max portfolio monthly contribution risk budget must be predefined.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Drawdown type: cyclical pullback or structural breakdown?
2. Fundamentals: improving, flat, or deteriorating?
3. Liquidity and solvency signals stable?
4. Is recovery probability high enough to justify new capital?

## Execution protocol
1. Set recurring orders.
2. Maintain DCA journal with reason codes.
3. Review average cost and thesis monthly.
4. Reassess contribution size quarterly.
