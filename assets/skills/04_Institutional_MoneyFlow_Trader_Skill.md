# Institutional Money-Flow Trader Skill

## Best fit
- Trader using sector-first, stock-second selection logic.
- Horizon: swing to position trades.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Institutions move sectors first, then leaders within sectors.
- Price and volume reveal accumulation/distribution earlier than narratives.

## Essential rules
1. Start with sector momentum ranking before single-stock screening.
2. Trade only stocks aligned with sector inflow direction.
3. Require at least 2 of 3 power moves for candidate quality.
4. Exit on institutional distribution signals.
5. Rotate capital when flow leadership changes.

## Risk guard rails
1. Do not hold laggards in weakening sectors.
2. Sector concentration cap <= 30% unless regime is exceptionally strong.
3. Per-position risk <= 1%.
4. Avoid illiquid names with poor execution quality.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Sector relative strength improving versus benchmark.
2. Stock volume spikes on up days.
3. Base/breakout structure with confirmation close.
4. Earnings or revenue acceleration confirms flow thesis.

## Execution protocol
1. Weekly: rank sectors.
2. Daily: monitor top-sector candidates for entry triggers.
3. On trigger: enter with predefined stop and size.
4. Reallocate promptly when flow rotates.
