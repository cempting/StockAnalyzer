# Sector Rotation Macro Investor Skill

## Best fit
- Investor replacing broad index passivity with active sector allocation.
- Horizon: multi-week to multi-quarter.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Leadership rotates by macro phase (rates, inflation, growth).
- Broad indices can hide concentration and valuation risk.

## Essential rules
1. Build sector scorecard and rank leadership monthly.
2. Use liquid sector ETFs for implementation.
3. Overweight top sectors, underweight laggards.
4. Use explicit exit trigger when sector leadership degrades.

## Risk guard rails
1. No single sector ETF > 25% unless tactical sleeve is separate.
2. Keep minimum diversification across at least 3 sectors.
3. Rebalance when top-ranked sector loses momentum regime.
4. Avoid chasing sectors after extreme short-term spikes.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Sector relative momentum across 1M/3M/6M windows.
2. Breadth within sector improving, not just one mega-cap.
3. Macro backdrop consistent with sector sensitivity.
4. Earnings revisions trend supportive.

## Execution protocol
1. Monthly: rerank sectors.
2. Weekly: monitor regime drift and trigger conditions.
3. Reallocate in tranches to reduce timing error.
4. Keep decision log of entry/exit rationale.
