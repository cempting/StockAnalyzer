# Market Collision Wave Investor Skill

## Best fit
- Macro investor following causal-chain capital flow waves.
- Horizon: thematic rotation over months.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Capital moves in waves through sectors as macro drivers propagate.
- Following the chain outperforms reacting to isolated headlines.

## Essential rules
1. Identify current wave position before buying stocks.
2. Prioritize sectors early in the active wave, avoid late-wave overcrowding.
3. Use sector scorecards to confirm wave progression.
4. Exit when wave leadership weakens or next wave starts.

## Risk guard rails
1. Avoid full allocation to a single wave stage.
2. Use staggered entries to handle false phase shifts.
3. Reduce gross exposure when causal chain breaks.
4. Keep a hedge or cash sleeve during transition phases.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Macro trigger appears (policy, rates, geopolitics, growth).
2. Sector performance sequence matches expected wave order.
3. Relative strength migrates from old leaders to new leaders.
4. Participation breadth expands in target sectors.

## Execution protocol
1. Build wave map and expected beneficiaries.
2. Validate with sector data weekly.
3. Deploy via sector baskets, then add top stock leaders.
4. Rotate out as leadership matures.
