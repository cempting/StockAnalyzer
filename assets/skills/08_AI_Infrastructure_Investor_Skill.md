# AI Infrastructure Investor Skill

## Best fit
- Thematic investor focused on picks-and-shovels beneficiaries.
- Horizon: multi-quarter trend participation.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Infrastructure captures demand regardless of which AI app/model wins.
- Capex cycles and institutional flow drive re-rating windows.

## Essential rules
1. Prioritize infrastructure tiers over speculative end-app winners.
2. Track capex acceleration and order-book strength.
3. Use institutional selling signals as risk-off warnings.
4. Size by volatility and cash-flow durability.

## Risk guard rails
1. Avoid overexposure to one sub-tier (chips, power, networking, services).
2. Cap single-name exposure in cyclical hardware names.
3. Trim into euphoric spikes when valuation disconnects from execution.
4. Watch inventory and margin compression risk.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Revenue growth plus margin resilience.
2. Sustained capex tailwind from hyperscalers/enterprise.
3. Relative strength leadership within AI infrastructure cohort.
4. Constructive base after pullback before adding.

## Execution protocol
1. Score candidates by tier and quality.
2. Build core basket, then add tactical leaders.
3. Reassess each earnings season for demand durability.
4. Rotate out of names showing institutional distribution.
