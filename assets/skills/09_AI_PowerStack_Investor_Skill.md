# AI Power-Stack Investor Skill

## Best fit
- Investor allocating across infrastructure, platform, and application layers.
- Horizon: thematic with risk-tiered sizing.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Different AI stack layers have different risk/reward and cycle timing.
- Layer diversification improves robustness versus single-layer bets.

## Essential rules
1. Define layer weights (conservative, balanced, aggressive) before entry.
2. Treat infrastructure as stability anchor.
3. Treat platform layer as growth core.
4. Treat application layer as controlled optionality.

## Risk guard rails
1. Application-layer exposure capped by risk profile.
2. Rebalance when layer drift exceeds threshold.
3. Avoid paying peak multiples for unproven monetization.
4. Demand evidence of adoption, retention, and unit economics.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Infrastructure: capex and supply constraints easing or monetizing.
2. Platform: model economics and enterprise demand broadening.
3. Application: repeatable revenue, not one-off hype spikes.
4. Cross-layer confirmation: leaders in multiple layers support theme durability.

## Execution protocol
1. Pick one of the 3 portfolio templates.
2. Build watchlist by layer.
3. Enter in tranches tied to earnings confirmation.
4. Rebalance quarterly or on regime break.
