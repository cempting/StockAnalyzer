# Space Catalyst Trader Skill

## Best fit
- Trader targeting high-upside thematic breakouts with strict validation.
- Horizon: event-driven swing to position trades.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Space names are narrative-sensitive and can move in bursts.
- Validity improves when technical and fundamental catalysts align.

## Essential rules
1. Require at least 2 of 3 power moves before entry.
2. Avoid IPO trap behavior (early hype without base).
3. Respect index rebalancing and forced-flow effects.
4. Prefer setups with clear catalyst calendar.

## Risk guard rails
1. Position size lower than broad-market names due to volatility.
2. Hard stop mandatory; no averaging down in broken momentum.
3. Limit aggregate thematic exposure to avoid correlated drawdowns.
4. Take partial profits into vertical moves.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Institutional volume spike.
2. Heartbeat/base pattern before expansion.
3. Record-quarter or fundamental inflection.
4. Post-catalyst follow-through with healthy volume.

## Execution protocol
1. Build catalyst watchlist.
2. Enter only on confirmation.
3. Trail stops as structure rises.
4. Exit on failed follow-through or distribution.
