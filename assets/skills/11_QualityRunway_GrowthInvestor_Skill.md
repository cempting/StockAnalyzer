# Quality Runway Growth Investor Skill

## Best fit
- Growth investor filtering for survivability + institutional tailwind + revenue inflection.
- Horizon: medium-to-long term.

## Market behavior assumptions
- In tighter liquidity regimes, runway and quality separate winners from fragile stories.
- Institutional sponsorship sustains trend durability.

## Essential rules
1. Apply 3 filters sequentially: runway, institutional support, revenue inflection.
2. Reject candidates failing any filter.
3. Prefer improving fundamentals over cheap narratives.
4. Combine filter pass with technical confirmation for timing.

## Risk guard rails
1. Avoid low-runway names with refinancing risk.
2. Avoid weak sponsorship in volatile markets.
3. Cap exposure to pre-profit companies.
4. Reduce size when growth quality is uncertain.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Cash runway adequate for macro stress window.
2. Institutional ownership trend stable/rising.
3. Revenue growth accelerating with improving quality.
4. Bonus: positive operating leverage or margin inflection.

## Execution protocol
1. Screen universe with strict filter logic.
2. Rank pass-list by quality and technical structure.
3. Enter in tranches with predefined invalidation.
4. Re-screen after each earnings cycle.
