# New-Fed Gold/Silver/Bitcoin Macro Trader Skill

## Best fit
- Macro trader allocating across precious metals, crypto, and miners under policy regime shifts.
- Horizon: tactical swings plus regime-based positioning.

## Market behavior assumptions
- Policy communication shocks can reprice non-yielding assets violently.
- Opportunity cost (real yields/cash yields) is a central driver.
- Fear premium expands/collapses quickly.

## Essential rules
1. Run 3-factor macro check before entries: policy stance, opportunity cost, fear premium.
2. Use staged entries only after stabilization.
3. Treat miners as setup-dependent, not narrative-dependent.
4. Define selling rules before buying.

## Risk guard rails
1. Per-trade risk <= 1%; high-beta sleeve <= 0.5-0.75%.
2. Avoid leverage around key policy events.
3. Cap combined macro-theme concentration.
4. Reduce gross exposure into uncertain regime transitions.

## Pattern recognition checklist
1. Bullion/crypto stabilization after liquidation.
2. Base-building and reclaim of trend structure.
3. Miners: sideways base + coiling + RS turn-up.
4. Confirmed follow-through volume after trigger.

## Execution protocol
1. Weekly macro score and bias update.
2. Enter in 3 tranches with improving confirmation.
3. Trail stops on winners and trim vertical spikes.
4. Exit immediately on thesis failure.
